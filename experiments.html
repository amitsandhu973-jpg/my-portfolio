CHAPTER 9: 10 ADVANCED PRINTING EXPERIMENTS

Practical Testing, Ink-Water Balance Analysis & Substrate Behaviour Trials

Verified By: Amit Sandhu (B.Tech)

Yeh section un 10 practical experiments par based hai jo ek professional printing engineer ya supervisor ko machine performance optimize karne ke liye pata hone chahiye:

EXPERIMENT 01 Dot Gain Measurement Trial

Alag-alag printing speed par 50% halftone dot ka gain check karna using a reflection densitometer to evaluate plate wear.

EXPERIMENT 02 Fountain Solution pH vs Emulsification

pH level ko 4.5 se 6.0 tak badal kar ink emulsification rate aur drying time par padne wale prabhav ka adhyayan karna.

EXPERIMENT 03 Cobb Absorption Test Trial

Alag-alag FBB aur WLC boards par 60-second water absorption test kar ke surface sizing quality ko verify karna.

EXPERIMENT 04 Ink Tack Sequence Audit

Wet-on-wet printing me back-trapping aur contamination rokne ke liye alag-alag process inks ki tack values ko test karna.

EXPERIMENT 05 Aqueous Coating Gloss vs Viscosity

Coating viscosity (Ford Cup No. 4) ko adjust karke gloss level aur rub resistance par padne wale asar ko maapna.

EXPERIMENT 06 Paper Acclimatization Study

Cold storage se nikli sheet ko bina conditioning ke chalane par hone wale register drift aur curled edges ka comparison trial.

EXPERIMENT 07 Delta-E Color Matching Trial

Spectrophotometer ki madad se pantone spot color ki wet vs dry Delta-E ($dE$) value me aane wale antar ko record karna.

EXPERIMENT 08 Roller Nip Stripe (Pressure) Test

Ink form rollers aur plate cylinder ke beech pressure stripe width (3-4mm) ko ink stripe method se test karna.

EXPERIMENT 09 UV Curing Energy Density Audit

UV lamp intensity aur conveyor speed ko badal kar coating cure hone ki poori saptahik (durability) aanchal ki jaanch karna.

EXPERIMENT 10 Creasing & Die-Cutting Crack Trial

Alag-alag board grain direction aur creasing matrix channel width ke hisab se folding ke dauran board fatne (cracking) ko rokne ka prayog.

💡 Amit Sandhu's Lab Rule:

Floor par koi bhi naya raw material ya chemical batch aane par, direct production shuru karne se pehle chote scale par yeh trials zaroor perform karein. Isse hazaron ruppe ka paper aur ink wastage bach jata hai!
