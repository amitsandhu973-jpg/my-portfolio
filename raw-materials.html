📜 1. Substrate, Grain Direction & Moisture Control

  • Grain Direction: मशीन की दिशा (Feed Direction) के समांतर ग्रेंस रखें। गलत ग्रेंस फोल्डिंग में क्रैक और रजिस्टर आउट (Misregistration) की जड़ है।
  • Moisture Control: बोर्ड/पेपर की नमी 4.5% - 5.5% होनी चाहिए। 24 घंटे प्रेस शॉप में Acclimatize करें।
  • RH Stability: प्रेस फ्लोर का RH 50% ± 5% रखें। बहुत ज्यादा ड्राई होने पर स्टेटिक और वेट एज (Wavy Edges) की समस्या आती है।
Defect Root Cause Corrective Action
Wavy Edges High Humidity (Paper absorbed moisture at edges) Dehumidify room; use side-air blast controls.
Tight Edges (Curl) Too Dry (Edges lost moisture to dry atmosphere) Wrap stacks in waterproof PE wrappers / Humidify room.

🛠️ 5. Master Floor Troubleshooting: Substrate to Coating Faults

Problem / Defect Root Cause Floor Action / Solution
Blistering (Coating bubbles) Board moisture trapped inside core + High IR heat intensity. Reduce IR lamp output; increase press running speed; enhance air knife blower volume.
Orange Peel Effect Coating viscosity too high or Anilox chamber pressure uneven. Adjust coating fluid viscosity with distilled water/additive; check doctor blade contact.
Blocking in Delivery Stack Insufficient drying/curing of coating before stack compression. Use anti-setoff powder, increase cooling fan speed, lower delivery pile weight.
Yellowing of Board/Coating UV lamp over-exposure or low-grade photo-initiator resins. Reduce active UV lamp power percentage; check yellowing resistance of varnish stock.
Static Jamming at Feeder Extremely dry paper stock combined with low relative humidity. Increase room relative humidity; activate active static elimination tinsel/bars.
