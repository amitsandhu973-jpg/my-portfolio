⚙️ 1. Packaging Substrate Technical Matrix (FBB, SBB, WLC)

Detailed structural breakdown of paperboards utilized in high-speed Heidelberg and Komori offset printing lines:

Board Grade Structure / Layers GSM Range Primary Packaging Application
GC1 (FBB) Multi-layer mechanical pulp center, chemical pulp outer layers, coated back. 200 - 450 GSM Pharma cartons, cosmetics, premium food boxes.

🔬 2. Standard Laboratory & Shop-Floor Testing Protocols

Cobb Value Test (ISO 535) Water Absorption

Measures the mass of water absorbed by 1 square meter of paper/board in a specified time (typically Cobb60 for 60 seconds).

Target Standard: 45 - 55 g/m² (For gloss carton boards to prevent ink setting issues & blistering).
Surface Smoothness (Parker Print-Surf) ISO 8791-4

Evaluates micro-roughness under simulated printing nip pressure. Lower micrometers indicate smoother surfaces.

Target Standard: < 1.2 µm for high-end halftone dot reproduction without pinholing.

🧪 3. Inline Coating Chemistry & Mechanics

Aqueous Coating (Dispersion Coating)

Applied via an anilox coater unit using water-based acrylic polymers. Viscosity is maintained typically between 35–45 seconds (Ford Cup No. 4 at 25°C). Provides anti-scuff protection, rapid drying via IR/Hot air dryers, and prevents sheet blocking in the delivery stack.

UV Coating & Primer Interaction

Ultraviolet curable coatings require total photo-initiator curing under high-intensity UV mercury/LED lamps. Proper energy density (mJ/cm²) is required to prevent migration risks in food-grade packaging. Base primers are essential when applying UV over non-absorbent metalized or synthetic substrates.

💡 Amit Sandhu's Production Floor Warning:

Never feed unconditioned cold-storage paper directly into high-speed printing nips. Temperature differentials between the pressroom and paper pallets cause moisture loss or gain along sheet edges, resulting in severe dimensional distortion, register drift, and creasing fractures during subsequent die-cutting operations. Always allow a 24-hour acclimatization period.
