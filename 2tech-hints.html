⚠️ 1. Print Defects & Root Cause Analysis (मशीन की दिक्कतें और उनके हल)

High-speed production (Heidelberg/Komori) ke waqt aane wale common defects aur unka turant solution:

Defect (समस्या) Symptom (पहचान) Root Cause (कारण) Corrective Action (समाधान)
Scumming (स्कमिंग) Plate ke non-image area me ink lagna aur paper par aana. Dampening water ki kami, pH/Conductivity ka galat hona. Fountain solution flow badhayein, pH (4.8-5.5) check karein.
Hickeys (हिकीज़) Print me chote-chote white rings (donut shape) aana. Ink skin ke sookhe tukde, paper dust, ya roller dirt. Fresh ink dalein, blanket wash karein, hickey picker use karein.
Slurring (स्लरिंग) Halftone dots gol ki jagah lambe (oval) dikhna. Cylinder ke bich over-packing, loose blanket. Blanket torque aur packing gauge se height check karein.
Set-Off (सेट-ऑफ) Niche wali sheet ki ink upar wali sheet ke piche lag jana. Heavy ink film, kam spray powder, slow drying. Spray powder volume badhayein, delivery pile ki height kam karein.

🔐 2. Amit Sandhu's Pro Floor Secrets & Hidden Technical Hints (सीक्रेट टिप्स)

💡 Secret #1: Ghosting Prevention Hint

Agar solid patch ya heavy background ke just baad light tone aa rahi hai aur Mechanical Ghosting ban rahi hai, toh form rollers ki axial oscillation (lateral movement) ko check karein. Agar rollers proper shift nahi ho rahe, toh ink distribution adjust karne ke liye form roller ko ek notch rotate kar dein ya dater roller par anti-ghosting device active karein.

💡 Secret #2: Quick Plate Swelling Trick

Emergency me agar plate par minor scratch aa jaye aur press rokani na pade, toh machine chalane se pehle cotton swab se mild plate cleaner ya gum arabic ki drop scratch par lagayein. Yeh non-image area ko protect karega aur bina job roke aage ke 200-300 impressions bacha lega.

⚡ Amit Sandhu's Diagnostic Golden Rule:

Machine par jab bhi koi defect aaye, toh andaza lagakar ek sath saari settings mat badlo. Hamesha EK HI PARAMETER change karo (jaise sirf pani badhana, ya sirf pressure kam karna) aur sheet nikal kar result dekho. Yahi ek professional supervisor ka sabse bada secret hai jisse wastages zero ho jati hain!
