Chapter 1: Advanced Machine Specifications, Cylinder Geometry & Shop-Floor Engineering Manual

1. Heidelberg Speedmaster CX 104 / SM 102 & Komori Lithrone Deep Technical Specs

• Operating Speed Thresholds: Heidelberg CX 104 is engineered for up to 16,500 sph (sheets per hour) on standard paper (60-250 GSM), but for heavy carton board (350-450 GSM), speed must be regulated between 12,000 to 13,500 sph to maintain perfect register and prevent centrifugal ink misting.

• Substrate Thickness Limits: Standard configuration handles 0.03 mm to 0.6 mm. Heavy packaging conversion kits allow processing up to 1.0 mm rigid folding box board (FBB) and micro-flute corrugated cardboard (E-flute / F-flute).

• Plate & Blanket Size Exact Standards:
- SM 74 Plate: $605 \text{ mm} \times 740 \text{ mm} \times 0.30 \text{ mm}$ thickness.
- CX 104 / CX 104 Plate: $790 \text{ mm} \times 1030 \text{ mm} \times 0.30 \text{ mm}$ thickness.
- Leading/Trailing Edge Clamping Bends: Standard 42 mm bend allowance on both sides to prevent plate stretch distortion.

2. Cylinder Undercut Geometry & Precise Packing Calculations

• Plate Cylinder Undercut (-0.15 mm to -0.30 mm): The plate packing calculation follows the formula: $\text{Packing Thickness} = \text{Undercut} + \text{Desired Height to Pitch Line}$. Standard base sheet packing is made of treated craft paper or polyester calibration sheets to eliminate dot distortion.

• Blanket Cylinder Undercut (-1.90 mm to -2.30 mm): Utilizes multi-ply compressible rubber blankets (overall thickness 1.95 mm to 1.96 mm including under-packing). If the blanket is over-packed by even +0.05 mm, it creates surface velocity mismatch, leading to "Slur" and premature plate wear.

• Impression Cylinder Hardness & Undercut: Undercut ranges from -0.20 mm to -0.40 mm with chrome-plated, corrosion-resistant surfaces designed to withstand heavy 400+ GSM board transfer pressure without indentation marks.

3. Ink Form Rollers & Dampening System Calibration (Stripe Testing)

• Ink Form Roller Stripe Widths: Checked via the standard ink stripe test (using 0.5 mm feeler gauges or ink bands).
- Soft Form Rollers (25° Shore A): Stripe width must be **5 mm to 6 mm**.
- Hard Form Rollers (30° to 35° Shore A): Stripe width must be **3 mm to 4 mm**.
Pressure must be perfectly uniform from Drive Side (DS) to Operator Side (OS) to prevent ghosting and starvation.

• Dampening System (Alcolor / Delta): Dampening form roller to plate stripe must be kept light at **2 mm to 3 mm**. Chrome metering roller to dampening form gap must maintain a precise micrometric film thickness of fountain solution to prevent emulsification.

4. Exclusive Shop-Floor Secrets & Mechanical Troubleshooting

• Gear Backlash & Slur Elimination: The internal gear mesh clearance (backlash) between the plate, blanket, and impression cylinder gears must strictly remain within **0.03 mm to 0.05 mm**. Excessive backlash results in high-speed mechanical vibration, manifesting as linear slur marks across the print.

• Feeder Head & Suction Dynamics for Heavy Board: When running high-caliper folding cartons (350–450 GSM), the forward-pushing pneumatic blast timing must be delayed by 4 to 6 degrees of the main drive cycle. The suction head speed vector must match the tangential speed of the feed board to avoid front-edge battering and double-sheet stops.

• Gripper Bite & Transfer Margin Rules: Standard gripper bite must be maintained at **8 mm to 10 mm**. Reducing bite below 7 mm in high-speed perfecting or heavy board units causes sheet flutter, leading to corner crashes and misregistration.

• Optical Register Encoder Maintenance: Micro-adjustment servo motors rely on high-precision optical encoders. Dust or ink mist accumulation on encoder discs causes register drift; cleaning with electronic-grade Isopropyl Alcohol (IPA) restores positioning accuracy to $\pm 0.002 \text{ mm}$ tolerance.
