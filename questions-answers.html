Q1: Offset printing me Ink-Water Balance ka mukhya siddhant kya hai?Dampening

Answer: Lithography ka basic rule hai 'Oil and Water do not mix'. Plate ka image area lipophilic aur non-image area hydrophilic hota hai.

Answer: IPA paani ka surface tension kam karta hai jisse paani plate par ek patli film ke roop me phailta hai aur cooling effect deta hai.

Q3: Fountain solution ka standard pH level kitna hona chahiye?Quality

Answer: Standard acidic range 4.8 to 5.5 honi chahiye taaki plate ka non-image area active rahe.
