💧 Part A: Ink-Water Balance & Dampening Mechanics (Q1 - Q30)

Q1: Offset printing me Ink-Water Balance ka mukhya siddhant kya hai?Dampening

Answer: Lithography ka basic rule hai 'Oil and Water do not mix'. Plate ka image area lipophilic (ink-loving) hota hai aur non-image area hydrophilic (water-loving) hota hai.

Q2: Dampening solution me IPA (Isopropyl Alcohol) ka kya kaam hai?Chemicals

Answer: IPA paani ka surface tension kam karta hai jisse paani plate par ek patli aur ek jaisi parat (film) ke roop me phailta hai aur cooling effect deta hai.

Q3: Fountain solution ka standard pH level kitna hona chahiye?Quality

Answer: Standard acidic range 4.8 to 5.5 honi chahiye taaki plate ka non-image area active rahe aur ink jaldi dry ho.

Q4 to Q30: Advanced Dampening TroubleshootingSummary Bank

Overview: Isme fountain solution ki conductivity (800-1200 µS/cm), emulsification percentage (ideal 15-25%), roller stripping, calcium buildup, chilled water recirculation, aur automated metering system ki poori technical detail shaamil hai.

📐 Part B: CTP Plates, Dimensions & Pre-Press Engineering (Q31 - Q60)

Q31: Heidelberg Speedmaster SM 74 ki plate ka standard size kya hai?Machine Specs

Answer: SM 74 machine ke liye standard plate ka size 605 mm x 740 mm hota hai.

Q32: CTP (Computer-to-Plate) technology me thermal plates kaise kaam karti hain?Pre-Press

Answer: Thermal CTP laser beam (aamtaur par 830nm wavelength) ka upyog karta hai jo exposed areas ki coating ko insoluble ya soluble banata hai, bina chemical processing ke bhi kai plates modern burner par chalti hain.

Q33 to Q60: Plate Wear & Dot ReproductionSummary Bank

Overview: Isme screen ruling (175-200 LPI), stochastic screening, plate baking techniques, grain structure, aur abrasion resistance se jude sabhi advanced sawal cover kiye gaye hain.

📦 Part C: Substrates, Board Mechanics & Cobb Testing (Q61 - Q90)

Q61: FBB (Folding Box Board) aur SBS me kya mukhya antar hai?Substrates

Answer: FBB me mechanical pulp center aur chemical pulp ki outer layers hoti hain, jabki SBS (Solid Bleached Board) poori tarah pure bleached chemical pulp se banta hai jo high-end luxury packaging ke liye use hota hai.

Q62: Cobb60 test ka kya mahatva hai?Lab Testing

Answer: Yeh 60 seconds me 1 square meter board dwara absorb kiye gaye paani ki matra (g/m²) ko maapta hai, jo ki ink setting aur coating absorption ke liye critical hai.

Q63 to Q90: Grain Direction & Climate AcclimatizationSummary Bank

Overview: Paper grain direction (long grain vs short grain), relative humidity control (45-55% RH), stiffness, bending resistance, aur die-cutting crease cracking se jude sabhi technical solutions.

⚙️ Part D: Press Mechanics, Rollers & Heidelberg/Komori Settings (Q91 - Q120)

Q91: Roller Nip Stripe (Pressure) ki standard width kitni honi chahiye?Mechanics

Answer: Form rollers aur plate cylinder ke beech ink stripe ki width aamtaur par 3mm se 4mm ke beech honi chahiye.

Q92: Blanket packing ka calculation kaise kiya jata hai?Calipers

Answer: Cylinder undercut depth me se blanket aur plate ki actual thickness ko ghata kar mikrometer se foil packing sheets (underpacking) ki exact gauge tay ki jati hai.

Q93 to Q120: Press Speeds & Register AccuracySummary Bank

Overview: Inline coating units, perfecting mechanisms, feeder suction head adjustments, sidelay/frontlay calibration, aur gear streak elimination ki poori master guide.

🚨 Part E: Advanced Troubleshooting, Defects & Pro Floor Secrets (Q121 - Q150+)

Q121: Print me 'Hickeys' aane ka mukhya root cause kya hai?Diagnostics

Answer: Paper dust, dried ink skins, ya ambient environment ke particles jab ink train se hote hue plate par jamte hain, toh hickeys bante hain.

Q122: Set-off (Back-side marking) ko rokne ke kya tarike hain?Delivery

Answer: Spray powder ka sahi volume, ink density ka control, aur delivery stack ki height ko kam rakhna taaki pressure se niche wali sheet ki ink kharab na ho.
