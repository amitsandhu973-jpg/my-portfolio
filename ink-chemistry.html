OFFSET INK:
Advanced Chemical Properties, Rheology & Formulation Parameters
🔬 1. Pigment Loading & Particle Size Metrology
SPECIFICATION DATA Pigment Concentration (% wt): Cyan (PB 15:3) mein 15%-22%, Magenta (PR 122) mein 18%-25%, Yellow (PY 12/13) mein 14%-20%, aur Black (PBk 7) mein 18%-24% pigment load hota hai.
Hegman Grind Gauge Index: High-end sheetfed inks ke liye particle size aggregation limit < 5 microns (Hegman 7.5 to 8.0) honi chahiye taaki plate scratching na ho.
🧪 2. Vehicle Chemistry & Rosin Ester Specs
POLYMER SCIENCE Phenolic Modified Rosin Esters: Gum rosin ko pentaerythritol ke sath esterify karke phenol resins ke sath co-condense kiya jata hai. Iska Acid Value 20-35 mg KOH/g aur Softening Point 135°C - 150°C hota hai.
High Boiling Petroleum Distillates: Solvent phase mein narrow boiling fractions (IBP 240°C aur FBP 290°C) use hote hain taaki press par open time maintain rahe.
📈 3. Rheological Equations & Viscoelasticity
RHEOLOGY PHYSICS Casson Fluid Model Parameters: Offset ink ek Non-Newtonian pseudoplastic fluid hai:
$$\sqrt{D} = \sqrt{D_0} + S \cdot \sqrt{V}$$ Yield value ($D_0$) ink ko rollers par sag hone se rokti hai.
Thixotropic Recovery Rate: Roller nip mein high shear par viscosity drop hokar 5-10 Pa·s aur rest par wapas 50-100 Pa·s ho jati hai.
⚡ 4. Inkometer Tack Numbers & Energy Transfer
MECHANICAL METRICS Thwing-Albert Inkometer Values (at 30°C, 400 RPM): Black: 18-22 | Cyan: 15-18 | Magenta: 12-15 | Yellow: 9-12 Tack Units. Yeh descending hierarchy wet-on-wet trapping ke liye zaroori hai.
🔥 5. Oxidative Cross-linking & Drier Catalysis
CHEMICAL DRYING Metallic Soap Catalysts: Primary drier ke roop mein Cobalt Naphthenate (surface drying) aur secondary drier ke roop mein Zirconium/Manganese (through drying) 0.05% - 0.2% concentration mein dale jate hain.
🌊 6. Surface Energy & Interfacial Tension
SURFACE CHEMISTRY Dynes / cm Balancing: Ink ki surface tension 32 - 38 dynes/cm aur fountain solution ki 38 - 42 dynes/cm rakhi jati hai taaki non-image area par ink na lage.
💎 7. Gloss Retention & Optical Density Parameters
OPTICAL PHYSICS Specular Gloss & Film Leveling: High gloss inks mein high molecular weight alkyd resins use hote hain jo drying ke dauran shrink nahi hote. Isse light specularly reflect hoti hai, aur standard process black ki optical density 1.65 - 1.85 DIN density tak achieve hoti hai.
🔄 8. Viscoelastic Storage & Loss Modulus (G' vs G'')
ADVANCED R&D Oscillatory Rheology Metrics: Rheometer testing par ink ka Storage Modulus (G') uske Loss Modulus (G'') se zyada hona chahiye (G' > G''). Iska matlab hai ki ink mein elastic solid property zyada strong hai, jo dot sharpness ko failne nahi deti.
🛡️ 9. Emulsion Rheology & Phase Inversion Threshold
ADVANCED R&D Water-in-Oil (W/O) Stability Window: Fountain solution ke sath milne par ink ko stable W/O emulsion banana padta hai. Agar water uptake 28% se 32% ki critical limit cross kar jaye, toh system Phase Inversion (Oil-in-Water) mein badal jata hai.
🧪 10. Low-Migration Chemistry & Food-Grade Compliance
PACKAGING R&D EuPIA Guidelines & Photoinitiator Limits: Food packaging aur pharmaceutical boxes ke liye banne wali inks mein mineral oils ki jagah modified vegetable oils aur non-migratory raw materials use hote hain. Iska migration limit threshold < 10 ppb (parts per billion) tak restricted hota hai taaki chemicals packaging se food product ke andar transfer na ho paayein.
⚡ 11. UV Monomer Reactivity & Free-Radical Curing
UV TECHNOLOGY Acrylate Functionality & Photo-Initiation: UV offset inks mein solvents ki jagah multifunctional acrylates ( jaise TMPTA aur DPHA) use hote hain. UV lamp ke exposure mein photo-initiators free radicals generate karte hain, jisse liquid ink ka solid polymer film mein convert hone ka time < 0.2 seconds lagta hai.
