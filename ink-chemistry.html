The Ultimate Engineering of Offset Printing Inks
Pro-Level Technical Masterclass: Chemistry, Rheology, Press Dynamics & Troubleshooting
📦 1. Advanced Chemical Composition & Formulation
Resin Matrix & Molecular Weight: Phenolic-modified rosin aur alkyd resins ka acid value ink ki hardness, gloss, aur water resistance tay karta hai. High-softening-point resins press speed high hone par film stability dete hain.
Solvent Boiling Ranges (240°C - 320°C): High-boiling petroleum distillates use hote hain taki open rollers par ink dry na ho, aur sirf paper par absorb ho.
Pigment Dispersion Indices: Particle size 0.1 se 1.0 micron tak hota hai, jisse transparent process colors ki CMYK overlapping behtareen milti hai.
🌊 2. Rheology & Viscosity Control
Viscosity Range (40 - 100 Poise): Sheetfed offset inks ke liye 25°C par yeh ideal range hai jo proper ink fountain flow banaye rakhti hai.
Pseudoplasticity (Shear Thinning): Ink ek non-Newtonian fluid hai; rollers ki high speed par yeh patli aur rest par aate hi gadi ho jati hai taki dot gain na ho.
⚡ 3. Tack Dynamics & Zippering Phenomenon
Inkometer Values (10 - 25 Units): 30°C par 400 RPM par tack measure ki jati hai. Multi-color mein sequence hamesha Descending Order (Black > Cyan > Magenta > Yellow) hota hai.
Critical Tack Limit: Agar paper ki pick resistance ink ke tack se kam ho, to high speed par paper surface ukhad jati hai (Picking/Plucking).
🔥 4. Drying Chemistry & Energy Systems
Oxidative Polymerization (Sheetfed): Unsaturated fatty acids hawa ke oxygen ke sath cross-link hokar solid 3D polymer film banate hain (Primary/Secondary driers ki madad se).
Evaporative & Penetration Drying: Heatset web mein 120-180°C ovens se solvent 0.2 seconds mein evaporate hota hai; coldset mein capillary action se absorb hoti hai.
📊 5. Spectrophotometric & Optical Properties
Wet Film Thickness & Density: Standard wet film thickness 0.8 se 1.2 micrometers hoti hai. Trapping efficiency ka formula:
E = [(D12 - D1) / D2] × 100 (Target > 80%)
💧 6. Ink-Water Balance & Emulsification
Optimum Water Uptake (15% - 25%): Fountain solution ke sath stable water-in-oil (W/O) emulsion banta hai. 30% se upar jaane par ink "drown" ho jati hai aur scumming aati hai.
☀️ 7. Pigment Lightfastness & Blue Wool Scale
Scale Rating (1 to 8): Pharmaceutical aur food packaging ke liye Scale 6 ya usse upar ki lightfastness wali inks use hoti hain taki dhoop ya light mein rang feeka na pade.
🛡️ 8. Rub Resistance & Slip Coefficient
PTFE & Amide Waxes: Transit ke dauran sheets ki ragad se bachne ke liye waxes milayi jati hain jo final scratch hardness aur rub resistance badhati hain.
🧪 9. Fountain Solution Chemistry & pH Impact
pH & Conductivity Balance: Fountain solution ka pH level 4.8 se 5.5 ke beech hona chahiye. Zyada acidic hone par ink ka drier neutralize ho jata hai aur drying ghanto delay ho sakti hai.
🌟 10. UV & EB Curing Ink Technology
Instant Photo-Polymerization: UV inks mein solvents ki jagah monomers aur oligomers hote hain jo UV light padte hi ek second ke hazareeqe hisse mein solid cure ho jate hain (No oxidation/evaporation).
🌱 11. Low-Migration & Eco-Friendly Formulations
Food-Grade Packaging Safety: EuPIA guidelines ke mutabiq food aur medicine boxes ke liye non-toxic, low-migration vegetable-oil-based inks use hoti hain taki harmful chemicals packaging cross karke product mein na jayein.
💨 12. Ink Misting & Press Environment Control
High-Speed Film Splitting: High-speed press par blanket se paper splitting ke dauran microscopic droplets hawa mein udti hain (Misting). Isse rokne ke liye electrostatic anti-misting agents use kiye jate hain.
