ADVANCED OFFSET INK DATA & CHEMISTRY
Formulation, Rheology, Pigment Physics & Technical Specifications
🧪 1. रासायनिक संरचना (Chemical Formulation Breakdown)
COMPONENTS BREAKDOWN Pigments (10% - 20%): Organic (Azo, Phthalo Blue, Rubine Red) aur Inorganic pigments jo tinting strength, opacity, aur light-fastness तय करते हैं। Particle size < 5 micron होना अनिवार्य है।
RESINS & BINDERS Binders / Varnishes (40% - 50%): Phenolic modified rosins, Maleic resins, aur Long-oil alkyds. Yeh pigments ko baandh kar rakhte hain aur paper par gloss film banate hain.
SOLVENTS & OILS Solvents / Mineral Oils (25% - 35%): High boiling point petroleum distillates (hydrocarbon oils) aur vegetable oils (soybean/linseed oil) jo rollers par ink ki flowability maintain karte hain.
📊 2. रियोलॉजिकल डेटा (Rheology & Viscosity Physics)
NON-NEWTONIAN BEHAVIOR Pseudoplasticity (Shear Thinning): शीटफेड इंक Non-Newtonian fluids hoti hain. Jab rollers high speed (shear force) par chalte hain, toh inki viscosity achanak kam ho jati hai (patli ho jati hai), aur rest par aate hi wapas gaadi ho jati hai.
VISCOSITY RANGE Viscosity Profile: Paste inks ki viscosity Poise ya Pascal-second (Pa·s) mein naapi jati hai. Sheetfed inks ki standard viscosity 40 to 100 Pa·s ke beech hoti hai (at 25°C).
📈 3. टैक् फिजिक्स और इंकमीट्री (Tack & Inkometer Data)
INKOMETER PARAMETERS Tack Measurement: Inkometer par 30°C temperature aur 400 RPM speed par 1 minute ke baad tack value record ki jati hai. Sheetfed inks ka standard tack 10 se 18 Units ke beech hota hai.
UNIT-TO-UNIT HIERARCHY Tack Gradient: Multi-color machine mein tack ka kram hamesha descending (ghatata hua) hona chahiye: Black (~16) > Cyan (~14) > Magenta (~12) > Yellow (~10), taaki pichhli ink agli unit par proper trap ho sake.
🔬 4. सुखाने की रासायनिक प्रक्रिया (Oxidative & Penetration Drying)
PHYSICAL PENETRATION Initial Setting (Absorption): Sabse pehle solvent ka ek hissa paper ke pores (capillaries) mein absorb hota hai, jisse ink paste se solid jaisi hone lagti hai (Setting phase).
OXIDATIVE POLYMERIZATION Final Curing (Oxidation): Unsaturated vegetable oils atmospheric oxygen ke sath react karke cross-link polymer network banate hain. Is process ko tez karne ke liye Cobalt (surface drier) aur Zirconium/Manganese (through drier) salts use kiye jate hain.
