OFFSET INK TECHNICAL RESEARCH
Advanced Polymer Rheology, Interfacial Chemistry & Plant Floor Parameters
🔬 1. Pigment Wetting & Oil Absorption (DBP Value)
R&D CHEMISTRY Dibutyl Phthalate (DBP) Absorption: Pigment particles ki porosity aur oil requirement check karne ke liye DBP absorption test hota hai (Standard: 60-90 ml per 100g). Agar pigment ki surface energy vehicle ke sath match na kare, toh 'Pigment Shock' lagta hai aur ink bikhakne lagti hai.
⚡ 2. Zeta Potential & Pigment Flocculation Stability
COLLOID SCIENCE Surface Charge Dynamics: Non-polar hydrocarbon vehicles mein bhi pigments ke upar microscopic charge hota hai. Zeta potential magnitude ±30 mV se zyada hone par hi particles aapas mein chipakte nahi hain, jisse ink ki shelf-life saalon tak safe rehti hai.
📈 3. Thixotropic Hysteresis Loop Area Dynamics
RHEOLOGY METRICS Structural Breakdown Energy: Rotational viscometer se nikala gaya Hysteresis Loop ka area batata hai ki roller train mein chalte waqt ink kitni jaldi patli hogi aur print hone ke baad turant wapas moti (stable) hogi taaki dot spread na ho.
🌊 4. Dynamic Surface Tension & Surfactant Migration
INTERFACIAL PHYSICS Milliseconds Lifetime Tension: Fountain solution aur ink ke milne par interfacial tension 10 se 15 dynes/cm ke beech honi chahiye. Agar surfactant ki migration speed slow hui, toh high speed press par emulsification fail ho jata hai.
🔥 5. Free-Radical Triplet State Kinetics in UV Inks
UV PHOTOCHEMISTRY Oxygen Inhibition Mitigation: UV curing ke dauran atmospheric oxygen free radicals ko neutralize kar deti hai. Isse bachne ke liye amine synergists use kiye jate hain jo triplet state photo-initiators ko proton transfer karke curing speed ko 0.1 second ke andar laate hain.
🎯 6. Surface Free Energy & Acid-Base Interactions
SURFACE ENERGETICS Lewis Acid-Base Components: Paper coating aur ink vehicle ke beech polar (acid-base) aur dispersive interactions hoti hain. Agar paper ki surface energy ink se kam hai, toh 'Ink Transfer Failure' yani unprinted pinholes aane lagte hain.
📊 7. Makro-Mottle and Micro-Mottle Spectroscopy
PRINT DEFECT ANALYSIS Uneven Absorption Density: Paper ke uneven formation ki wajah se solvent absorption alag-alag speed par hota hai, jisse solid area mein dhabbe (mottle) aate hain. Iske liye high molecular weight resins use kiye jate hain jo pore plugging ko control karte hain.
⚙️ 8. Roller Nip Heat Dissipation & Viscosity Index
MECHANICAL THERMODYNAMICS Viscosity-Temperature Coefficient: Press ki speed badhne par shear heat se ink ka temperature 45°C tak jane par viscosity 60% tak gir sakti hai. High-end inks mein special polymers hote hain jo temperature badhne par bhi viscosity maintain rakhte hain.
💧 9. HLB Value (Hydrophilic-Lipophilic Balance) in Emulsions
EMULSION SCIENCE W/O Balance Control: Offset inks ke resins aur additives ka HLB value 3 se 6 ke beech rakha jata hai taaki woh lipophilic (oil loving) rahein aur fountain solution ko 25% tak hi apne andar hold karein. Isse zyada hone par ink emulsify hokar rollers se utar jayegi.
🧪 10. Iodine Value & Drying Oil Unsaturation Index
OXIDATIVE CHEMISTRY Double Bond Concentration: Linseed aur Tung oil ki drying capacity unke Iodine Value (130 se 180 g I2 per 100g) par depend karti hai. Jitne zyada conjugated double bonds honge, oxygen absorption utna hi fast hoga aur film utni hi mazboot banegi.
💎 11. Refractive Index Matching & Gloss Maximization
OPTICAL PHYSICS RI Synchronization: Pigment particles aur cured resin matrix ka Refractive Index (lagbhag 1.55 se 1.60) aapas mein match hona chahiye. Agar refractive index match nahi hoga, toh light scatter ho jayegi aur gloss dead ho jayega.
🛠️ 12. Tack-O-Scope Splitting Force & Filamentation Dynamics
RHEOLOGICAL STRESS Ink Mist Generation (Fly): High-speed presses par roller nip se ink split hote waqt microscopic filaments (threads) banate hain jo tutkar ink mist (dhuan/fly) banate hain. Iska control ink ki elasticity aur high molecular weight fractions ke balance par hota hai.
🛡️ 13. Rub Resistance & Coefficient of Friction (COF)
POST-PRESS METRICS Slip Additives & Wax Synergy: Printed surface ki scratch resistance ke liye micronized PE (Polyethylene) waxes use hoti hain jo static aur kinetic COF ko 0.2 se 0.3 tak laati hain, jisse post-press binding mein scratching nahi hoti.
🌡️ 14. Glass Transition Temperature (Tg) of Resin Vehicles
POLYMER PHYSICS Thermal Softening Point: Rosin ester aur alkyd blend ka Glass Transition Temperature (Tg) 45°C se 65°C ke beech design kiya jata hai taaki press par chalne ke baad ink room temperature par turant solid hard film mein convert ho jaye.
🔍 15. Spectrophotometric Delta E00 & Spectral Reflectance
COLOR METROLOGY CIEDE2000 Color Difference Formula: Visual color match ke alawa spectral curve matching ki jati hai. High-end packaging mein Delta E value 1.0 se kam honi chahiye taaki batch-to-batch color variation bilkul zero rahe.
⚡ 16. Fountain Solution Conductivity & Electrochemical Balance
ELECTROCHEMISTRY Ion Concentration Monitoring: Fountain water mein dissolved salts ki conductivity 800 se 1200 microSiemens/cm honi chahiye. Ion balance bigadne par ink ke driers neutralize ho jate hain aur printing dry nahi hoti.
⏳ 17. Penetration Porosity Dynamics & Wash-In Prevention
FLUID MECHANICS Lucas-Washburn Capillary Flow: Paper ke capillaries mein ink vehicle ke ghusne ki speed Lucas-Washburn equation se govern hoti hai. Agar penetration bahut fast ho jaye, toh vehicle andar chala jata hai aur pigment surface par bina bind kiye reh jata hai (Chalking defect).
✨ 18. Metallic Flake Orientation & Angular Metallic Flop
SPECIALTY R&D Specular Angular Reflection: Gold aur Silver metallic inks mein aluminum flakes ki thickness 0.5 micron aur diameter 10-20 micron hota hai. Inhe wet film mein perfectly parallel align hona padta hai taaki viewing angle badalne par metallic flop (chamak) proper dikhe.
