ULTIMATE OFFSET INK MANUAL
Complete Composition, All 18 R&D Parameters & Press-Floor Checkpoints
🧪 1. Ink Composition & Core Principles
COMPONENTS Raw Materials: Organic/Inorganic Pigments, Vehicles (Phenolic Modified Rosin Esters & Alkyd Resins), High-boiling Petroleum/Vegetable Oils, aur Metallic Driers/PE Waxes.
LITHOGRAPHY Principle & Drying: Oil aur water repulsion सिद्धांत। Drying dual mechanism se hoti hai—Pehle solvent paper mein absorb hota hai (Setting), fir oils oxygen ke sath oxidize hokar polymer film banate hain (Oxidation).
🔬 2. Advanced R&D Technical Parameters (All 18 Points)
POINT 01 DBP Absorption: Pigment porosity check karne ke liye (Standard: 60-90 ml/100g). Match na hone par Pigment Shock lagta hai.
POINT 02 Zeta Potential: Surface charge ±30 mV se zyada hone par particles aapas mein nahi chipakte, jisse shelf-life safe rehti hai.
POINT 03 Thixotropic Hysteresis Loop: Roller train mein chalte waqt ink ka patla hona aur print ke turant baad wapas dot spread rokne ke liye mota hona.
POINT 04 Dynamic Interfacial Tension: Fountain solution aur ink ke beech tension 10-15 dynes/cm. Slow migration se emulsification fail hota hai.
POINT 05 UV Triplet State Kinetics: UV inks mein oxygen inhibition se bachne ke liye amine synergists use kiye jate hain.
POINT 06 Surface Free Energy: Lewis acid-base interactions. Paper energy kam hone par Ink Transfer Failure (pinholes) aate hain.
POINT 07 Makro & Micro Mottle: Uneven absorption ki wajah se solid area mein dhabbe. High molecular weight resins se control hota hai.
POINT 08 Roller Heat Dissipation: High speed par shear heat se temperature badhne par viscosity maintain rakhne wale polymers.
POINT 09 HLB Value (Hydrophilic-Lipophilic): 3 se 6 range taaki ink lipophilic rahe aur 25-30% fountain solution hold kare.
POINT 10 Iodine Value: Linseed/Tung oil ki drying capacity (130-180 g I2). Conjugated double bonds se oxygen absorption fast hota hai.
POINT 11 Refractive Index Matching: Pigment aur resin matrix ka RI (1.55-1.60) match hone par gloss maximize hota hai.
POINT 12 Tack-O-Scope & Filamentation: High speed par nip split hone se banne wale filaments aur ink mist (fly) ka control.
POINT 13 Rub Resistance & COF: Micronized PE waxes se static aur kinetic COF 0.2-0.3 tak laakar scratching roki jati hai.
POINT 14 Glass Transition Temperature (Tg): Resin ka thermal softening point (45-65°C) taaki room temp par hard film bane.
POINT 15 Spectrophotometric Delta E00: CIEDE2000 formula se batch-to-batch color variation (< 1.0) maintain ki jati hai.
POINT 16 Fountain Conductivity: Dissolved salts ki conductivity 800-1200 microSiemens/cm taaki driers neutralize na ho.
POINT 17 Lucas-Washburn Capillary Flow: Paper capillaries mein penetration speed; fast hone par Chalking defect aata hai.
POINT 18 Metallic Flake Orientation: Gold/Silver inks mein aluminum flakes ka parallel alignment taaki metallic flop proper dikhe.
🛠️ 3. Press-Floor Running Machine Checkpoints
CHECKPOINT 01 Emulsification & Water Balance: Print dull ya creamy hone par dampening water kam karein. Conductivity aur pH (4.8-5.2) check karein.
CHECKPOINT 02 Trapping & Tack Sequence: Multi-color units mein tack hierarchy (Black > Cyan > Magenta > Yellow) maintain karein taaki back-trapping na ho.
CHECKPOINT 03 Blanket Piling & Linting: Paper dust jamne par gel reducer se tack down karein aur blanket wash karein.
CHECKPOINT 04 Hickies & Skinning: Ring-shaped spots aur dry ink skins ke liye plate/blanket wash karein aur fountain clean rakhein.
CHECKPOINT 05 Ghosting & Roller Stripping: Form roller pressure (strip width) check karein. Steel rollers par water film aane par deglazer use karein.
CHECKPOINT 06 Drying, Chalking & Slur: Delivery daag rokne ke liye powder spray badhayein, drier levels check karein, aur pH ko 4.0 se niche na jane dein. Blanket packing aur gripper tight rakhein.
