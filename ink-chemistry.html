Proprietary R&D Rheology, Molecular Ink Chemistry & Plant-Floor Diagnostics
🧪 1. Molecular Architecture & Raw Chemistry
POLYMER MATRIX Phenolic Modified Rosin Esters & Alkyd Backbone: High-end sheetfed inks mein pentaerythritol esterified rosins use hoti hain jinki molecular weight distribution (Mw/Mn) 15,000 se 25,000 Dalton hoti hai. Yeh matrix pigment ko encase karke high gloss aur scratch resistance deti hai.
High-Boiling Distillates & Vegetable Oils: 240°C se 300°C boiling range ke aliphatic hydrocarbons aur conjugated Linseed/Tung oils ka blend, jo rollers par shear thinning maintain karta hai aur press roke bina 72 ghante tak open rehta hai.
🔬 2. Advanced Rheology & Shear-Thinning Physics
NON-NEWTONIAN BEHAVIOR Thixotropic Hysteresis Loop Area: Rotational viscometer par test karne par ink ka structural breakdown energy curve define hota hai. Roller train ke high shear zone (10,000 sec⁻¹) mein viscosity gir kar 5 Poise reh jati hai, aur plate par aate hi wapas 150 Poise ho jati hai taaki dot gain na ho.
Zeta Potential & Pigment Flocculation: Non-polar vehicle mein Pigment Agglomeration rokne ke liye surface charge ±30 mV se upar rakha jata hai. Agar dispersion fail hui, toh ink mein micro-clumps banenge jo direct printing plate ko scratch kar denge.
⚡ 3. Surface Energy & Fountain Solution Thermodynamics
INTERFACIAL TENSION Dynamic Interfacial Tension (10-15 dynes/cm): Fountain solution aur ink ke beech ka contact angle aur interfacial tension agar mismanaged ho, toh high speed (15,000 sheets/hour) par emulsion phase inversion ho jata hai aur ink fountain mein cream ban kar beh jati hai.
Lewis Acid-Base Paper Interaction: Paper coating ki surface energy aur ink vehicle ki polar/dispersive components ka mismatch hone par 'Unprinted Pinholes' (Ink Transfer Failure) aur micro-mottling hoti hai.
🛠️ 4. Plant Floor Diagnostics: Tack, Trapping & Kinetics
TACK HIERARCHY & TRAPPING Inkometer Tack Sequencing: Multi-color Heidelberg/Komori par unit-to-unit tack drop mathematically 1.5 to 2.0 units hona chahiye (e.g., Black: 18 -> Cyan: 16 -> Magenta: 14 -> Yellow: 12). Agar sequence ulta hua, toh pehli unit ki ink pichhe khinch kar blanket par aa jayegi (Back-Trapping / Rejection).
OXIDATIVE CROSS-LINKING Cobalt-Zirconium Drier Kinetics: Sheet delivery par drying do phase mein hoti hai—Pehle solvent capillary action se paper mein ghusta hai (Lucas-Washburn flow), fir Cobalt (surface drier) aur Zirconium (through drier) atmospheric oxygen ke sath double bonds ko cross-link karke solid polymer banate hain. Agar pH < 4.0 hua, toh driers neutralize ho jayenge aur chalking hogi.
⚙️ 5. Press-Floor Critical Troubleshooting Matrix
DEFECT ELIMINATION Hickies & Roller Stripping Root Cause: Ring-shaped hickies paper calcium carbonate fillers aur dry ink skins ki wajah se aati hain. Steel rollers par water film (Stripping) aane ka matlab hai roller deglazing ki zaroorat hai aur fountain conductivity 1000 µS/cm se bahar ja chuki hai.
Ghosting & Slur Rectification: Mechanical ghosting form roller strip width (minimum 4-5mm solid contact) aur gear wear ki vajah se hoti hai. Blanket packing gauge aur squeeze pressure (0.05mm tolerance) check karein.
