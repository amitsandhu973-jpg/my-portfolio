OFFSET INK MASTER GUIDE
From Basic Foundation to Advanced R&D & Press Floor Diagnostics
🟢 1. Basic Foundation: What is Offset Ink?
BASICS - STEP 1 Ink ki Biyologia (Composition): Offset ink koi aam rang nahi hai, yeh ek gaadi paste (viscous paste) hai jo 4 cheezon se banti hai: Pigments (Rang ke liye mitti ya chemicals), Vehicles (Resins aur oils jo rang ko baandh kar rakhte hain), Solvents (Jo rollers par ink ko geela rakhte hain), aur Additives (Driers aur Waxes jo jaldi sukhane aur chamkane ke kaam aate hain).
BASICS - STEP 2 Lithographic Principle (Tel aur Paani ka Niyam): Offset printing ka mool siddhant yeh hai ki 'Tel aur Paani aapas mein milte nahi hain'. Printing plate par image area tel (ink) ko pakadta hai aur non-image area paani (fountain solution) ko pakadta hai.
📊 2. Intermediate Technical Data (Tack, Viscosity & Density)
TECHNICAL DATA - 1 Tack (Chipchipaapan / Khinchav Force): Inkometer par 30°C aur 400 RPM par napha jata hai. Multi-color machine mein unit-to-unit tack ka utarna zaroori hai (jaise Black sabse zyada chipchipi, fir Cyan, Magenta aur sabse kam Yellow). Agar yeh sequence bigda, toh paper ya pehli ink ukhad kar blanket par aa jayegi.
TECHNICAL DATA - 2 Optical Density & Viscosity: Standard Process Black ki print density 1.65 se 1.85 DIN honi chahiye. Viscosity ek aisi property hai jisme machine ke rollers ki high speed (shear force) milne par ink patli ho jati hai aur rest par aate hi wapas moti ho jati hai (Pseudoplastic nature).
TECHNICAL DATA - 3 Hegman Grind (Particle Size): High-end sheetfed inks mein pigment ke kan (particles) ka size 5 micron se kam hona chahiye, nahi toh plate par scratch pad jayenge aur fine dots cut jayenge.
🔬 3. Advanced R&D Parameters (Deep Industrial Data)
ADVANCED R&D - 1 Thixotropic Hysteresis Loop & Zeta Potential: Rotational viscometer se nikala gaya loop area batata hai ki rollers mein chalte waqt ink kitni jaldi energy todti hai. Pigment stability ke liye Zeta Potential ±30 mV se upar hona chahiye taaki particles aapas mein na chipkein.
ADVANCED R&D - 2 Interfacial Tension & HLB Balance: Fountain solution aur ink ke beech ki tension 10-15 dynes/cm honi chahiye. Ink ka HLB value 3 se 6 ke beech rakha jata hai taaki woh lipophilic rahe aur 25% tak paani (emulsion) hold kar sake.
🛠️ 4. Press Floor Running Machine Checkpoints
MACHINE FLOOR - 1 Emulsification & Water-Ink Balance Check: Agar print dull ho raha hai ya fountain mein creamy foam ban gaya hai, toh dampening water bahut zyada hai. Fountain solution ki conductivity (800-1200 µS/cm) aur pH (4.8-5.2) turant check karein.
MACHINE FLOOR - 2 Hickies & Blanket Piling Check: Sheet par gol ring jese nishane (hickies) paper dust ya dry ink skins ki wajah se aate hain. Blanket par white powder jamne par ink ka tack 'Gel Reducer' dalkar kam karein.
MACHINE FLOOR - 3 Drying & Chalking Check: Delivery pile mein sheet ke pichhe daag lagna (offsetting) ya ungli lagane par rang utarna (chalking) is baat ka sanket hai ki drier (Cobalt/Zirconium) kam hai ya fountain ka pH bahut niche (< 4.0) gir gaya hai. Anti-set-off powder spray aur stack height adjust karein.
