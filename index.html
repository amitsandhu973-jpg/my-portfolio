Offset Printing Knowledge Base

1. Raw Materials & Consumables

Art Paper

Specs: 80-300 GSM, Gloss/Matt

Use: High quality multi-color print.

Issue: Picking/Surface peeling.

Solution: Reduce tack of ink.

Offset Ink (Sheetfed)

Specs: Oxidizing, Mineral/Veg based

Use: CMYK Image transfer.

Issue: Chalking/Slow drying.

Solution: Add Drier/Check temperature.

Rubber Blanket

Specs: 1.95mm compressible

Use: Plate to Paper transfer.

Issue: Smash/Indentations.

Solution: Repack cylinder.

2. Pressroom Troubleshooting

Hickies

Cause: Dust/Dried ink particles.

Solution: Clean duct/blanket, check suction.

Ghosting

Cause: Uneven ink distribution.

Solution: Adjust roller pressure/stroke.

Moiré

Cause: Screen angle interference.

Solution: Change screen angles.

Scumming

Cause: Plate blinded.

Solution: Adjust pH level/Gum plate.
