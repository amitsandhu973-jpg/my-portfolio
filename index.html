5. Quality Control (QC) & Laboratory Testing Equipment

To ensure high quality standards across all packaging materials, the following quality assurance tests and equipment are utilized:

1. Scuff / Rub Resistance Tester

  • Purpose: Evaluates the abrasion and rub resistance of printed inks and coatings during transport.
  • Testing Parameters: Operates at 60 RPM with a 2 LBS deadweight attached.
  • Standard Test Cycles: 750 Rubs for UV Coated boards | 500 Rubs for Aqueous / Water-based Coated boards.

2. GSM (Grams per Square Meter) Tester & Electronic Balance

  • Purpose: Measures the weight density of paper, folding box board (FBB), and duplex board.
  • Sample Preparation: 10cm x 10cm (100 cm²) precision template cutter.
  • Calculation Formula:
GSM = (Weight of 100 cm² Sample in Grams) × 10,000 / 100

3. Taber Stiffness Tester

  • Purpose: Evaluates the bending resistance, stiffness, and elasticity of paperboard to ensure smooth runnability on high-speed automatic packaging lines.
  • Measurement Units: Taber Stiffness Units (g·cm or mN·m).

4. Bursting Strength Tester

  • Purpose: Measures the maximum hydraulic pressure a paperboard or corrugated board can withstand before rupturing.
  • Working Principle: Uses a rubber diaphragm inflated by glycerin fluid against a clamped circular specimen area.
  • Unit of Measurement: kg/cm² or kPa.

5. ECT / RCT / FCT Testing Machine (Corrugation Lab)

  • ECT (Edge Crush Test): Measures the cross-direction crushing strength of corrugated board edges (Determines box stacking strength).
  • RCT (Ring Crush Test): Measures the edgewise compression strength of paper strips used for fluting and liners.
  • FCT (Flat Crush Test): Measures the resistance of corrugated flutes against flat crushing force.

6. Cobb Moisture Absorption Tester

  • Purpose: Measures the amount of water absorbed by paper or paperboard in a specified time interval (usually 60 seconds or 180 seconds).
  • Formula: Cobb Value = (Weight after exposure - Dry weight) × 100 [Expressed in g/m²].

7. Ford Cup Viscosity Meter

  • Purpose: Measures the flow viscosity of liquids such as printing inks, aqueous coatings, UV varnishes, and adhesives.
  • Method: Measures the exact time in seconds (using a stopwatch) for liquid to drain completely through a standardized orifice (Ford Cup #4).

8. Digital Moisture Meter

  • Purpose: Measures the percentage of moisture retained inside paper, paperboard, and corrugated sheets to prevent warping and cracking at creases.
  • Standard Ideal Moisture Limits:
    • Virgin Paper / Board: 6% to 8%
    • Corrugated Fluted Boards: 6% to 10%
