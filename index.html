2. In-Depth Machine Sub-Systems & Mechanical Operations

A. Feeder Unit Breakdown

  • • Suction Head: 4 Lifting Suckers (for sheet lift) & 5 Forwarding Suckers (for sheet transport).
  • • Air Blowers & Separation: 17 Air Blower Nozzles + 6 Flexible Steel Finger Separators.
  • • Rear Edge Stoppers: 2 Units, precision set 1 mm away from the trailing edge of paper stack.
  • • Stack Level Height: Maintained 5–8 mm below the forwarding flap.
  • • Runners & Lays: 2 Forwarding Runners, 4 Transferring Runners, 2 Mechanical/Pneumatic Side Lays, 18 Front Lay Pins.
  • • Gripper Systems: Swing Arm Gripper with 18 Gripper Fingers. Ultrasonic Double Sheet Sensor integrated.

B. Inking & Dampening Roller Technical Specs

  • • 4 Ink Form Roller Diameters: White Roller (⌀ 60 mm), Blue Roller (⌀ 72 mm), Red Roller (⌀ 66 mm), Yellow Roller (⌀ 80 mm).
  • • Transfer & Oscillating Rollers: 10 Transfer Rollers (5 Rilsan-coated hard rollers + 5 Rubber rollers), 4 Oscillating Rollers (⌀ 85 mm).
  • • Dampening System Configuration: Pan Roller (⌀ 108 mm), Stainless Steel Metering Roller (⌀ 98 mm), Dampening Form Roller (⌀ 78 mm), Chromium Vario Roller (⌀ 85 mm).

C. Delivery System & Temperature Controls

  • • Delivery Grippers: 14 Gripper Bars (each bar holding 18 gripper fingers).
  • • Air Blowing & Slowdown: 25 Air Blow-down Fans + 5 Suction Slow-down Discs.
  • • Powder Spray Unit: 8 Spray Nozzles (Application Ratio: 80% Uncoated Paper / 20% Coated Paper).
  • • Drying & Temperature Standards: Protective Varnish (32°C – 34°C), Ink & Gloss Varnish (36°C), High Gloss Varnish (36°C – 38°C).

3. Printing Chemistry, Dampening & Coating Technologies

Fountain Solution & Water Parameters

  • • Standard Mix: 86% Water + 2–4% Fountain Solution Additive + Isopropyl Alcohol (IPA).
  • • pH Control Range: 4.5 – 5.5 (Acidic range to keep non-image area clean).
  • • Chiller Temperature: 8°C – 10°C.
  • • Electrical Conductivity: 800 – 2000 µS/cm (For UV Inks: 800 – 2500 µS/cm).

Offset Ink Composition & Viscosity

  • • Raw Materials: Pigment (Colorant), Synthetic Resins (Binder), Vegetable Oils/Mineral Oils (Vehicles), Driers, Solvents, Performance Additives.
  • • Tack & Viscosity Range: High Viscosity Paste (40 – 100 Pa·s).

4. Quality Control (QC) Lab Testing & Standard Formulas

Testing EquipmentQuality ParameterCalculation Formula & Operating Standards
Rub / Scuff Tester Ink & Coating Rub Resistance Operated at 60 RPM with 2 lbs weight.
• Aqueous Standard: Minimum 500 rubs.
• UV Standard: Minimum 750 rubs.
GSM Tester Grammage (Paper/Board Weight) Formula:
GSM = (Weight of sample in grams / Area in cm²) × 10,000
Taber Stiffness Tester Bending Resistance / Stiffness Formula:
Stiffness (MD) = [(Left Reading + Right Reading) × 5] / 2
Conversion: mN = 2.04 × Taber Unit
Bursting Strength Tester Substrate Rupture Limit Formula:
Burst Factor = (Bursting Strength in kg/cm² / Dry GSM) × 1000
Cobb Tester Water Absorption Capacity Formula:
Cobb Value = Weight after water exposure (g) - Initial Dry Weight (g) (Calculated for 100cm² area for 60/120 sec).
B4 Ford Cup Coating & Varnish Viscosity Measured in Efflux Time (Seconds). Standard flow time recorded at 25°C temperature.
Moisture Meter Substrate Moisture Percentage • Paper & Folding Box Board Standard: 6% – 8%
• Corrugated Fluted Board Standard: 6% – 10%
