4. Surface Coating & Drying Systems

Types of Coatings & Applications

  • Aqueous Coating: Water-based, smudge-resistant, quick drying (2 to 4 g/m²).
  • Varnish Coating: Low cost, available in Gloss/Matte/Satin (2 to 4 g/m²).
  • Primer Coating: Base coat for UV adhesion improvement (1.5 to 4 g/m²).
  • Soft Touch Coating: Velvet texture, luxurious tactile finish (3 to 4 g/m²).
  • UV Coating: High gloss, cured under UV light, excellent liquid/scuff resistance (3 to 10 g/m²).

6. Quality Control (QC) & Testing Equipment

  • Scuff / Rub Resistance Tester: Evaluates print rub proofness (60 RPM, 2 LBS weight; 750 rubs for UV, 500 rubs for Aqueous).
  • GSM Tester & Weighing Scale: Calculates paper/board weight $(GSM = \frac{Weight (g)}{Area (cm^2)} \times 10,000)$.
  • Taber Stiffness Tester: Evaluates material flexural strength and elasticity.
  • Bursting Strength Tester: Measures hydraulic load resistance using glycerin.
  • ECT / RCT / FCT Tester: Edge, Ring, and Flat Crush Testing for corrugated paperboards.
  • Cobb Tester: Measures water absorbency over 60s/180s.
  • Ford Cup: Viscosity measurement in seconds.
  • Moisture Meter: Checks moisture % (Ideal: Paper 6-8%, Corrugated Board 6-10%).
