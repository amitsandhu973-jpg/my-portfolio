Dampening & Fountain Solution Target Parameters

pH Level Targets

  • Standard Range: 4.8 – 5.5 pH
  • Buffer Control: Maintains ink-water balance.
  • Low pH (< 4.5): Slows ink drying, strips roller.
  • High pH (> 5.8): Causes ink emulsification & scumming.

Conductivity & IPA

  • Conductivity Target: 800 – 1500 µS/cm
  • IPA (Alcohol) Range: 6% – 10%
  • Fresh Water Base: 200 – 300 µS/cm target.
  • Monitoring: Daily bath tank check & replacement.

Chiller Temperature

  • Dampening Temp: 10°C – 12°C
  • Cylinder Packing: Precise paper/polyester underpacking.
  • Impression Clearance: Caliper based setting.
Print Quality & Troubleshooting Protocols

Defect Prevention

  • Hickies / Spotting: Roller cleaning & ink dust check.
  • Scumming / Toning: Adjust fountain solution & roller pressure.
  • Ghosting: Adjust ink form roller sweep & oscillation.
  • Set-off: Optimize anti-setoff powder spray & pile height.

Color & TVI Targets

  • Dot Gain (TVI): Managed according to ISO 12647-2 standards.
  • Registration: Electronic register & optical mark alignment.
  • Densitometry: Density control across C, M, Y, K zones.
