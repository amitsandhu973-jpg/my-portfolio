03. Printing Chemistry & Inline Coating Standards

Fountain Solution Controls

  • Chemical Recipe: 86% Water + 2–4% Fountain Solution + IPA
  • pH Target Range: 4.5 – 5.5 (Acidic Balance)
  • Chiller Temperature: 8°C – 10°C
  • Conductivity Limit: 800 – 2000 µS/cm (UV: 800 – 2500 µS/cm)

Offset Inks & Anilox Parameters

  • Ink Ingredients: Pigment, Synthetic Resins, Vehicles, Driers
  • Ink Viscosity: High Paste (40 – 100 Pa·s)
  • Anilox Rollers: Ceramic Plasma & Chromium Anilox
  • Cell Angles: 45° and 60° Hexagonal/Pyramid Cells
  • Doctor Blade Angle: Set between 55° and 65°

04. Quality Control (QC) Lab Testing & Formulas

Test EquipmentTested ParameterCalculation Formula & Test Standard

• Aqueous Target: Min 500 rubs
• UV Target: Min 750 rubs
GSM Tester Paper / Board Grammage GSM = (Weight in Grams / Area in cm²) × 10,000
Taber Stiffness Tester Bending Resistance Stiffness (MD) = [(Left + Right) × 5] / 2
Conversion: mN = 2.04 × Taber Unit
Bursting Tester Substrate Rupture Limit Burst Factor = (Bursting Strength / Dry GSM) × 1000
Cobb Tester Water Absorption Capacity Cobb = Weight After Exposure - Initial Dry Weight
B4 Ford Cup Coating Viscosity Efflux Time in Seconds recorded at 25°C standard.
