04. Quality Control (QC) Lab Testing & Formulas

Test Equipment Tested Parameter Calculation Formula / Operational Standard
Rub / Scuff Tester Ink Rub Resistance 60 RPM @ 2 lbs weight.
• Aqueous Target: Min 500 rubs | UV Target: Min 750 rubs
GSM Tester Grammage (g/m²) GSM = (Weight in Grams / Area in cm²) × 10,000
Taber Stiffness Tester Bending Resistance Stiffness (MD) = [(Left + Right) × 5] / 2  |  mN = 2.04 × Taber Unit
Bursting Tester Substrate Rupture Limit Burst Factor = (Bursting Strength / Dry GSM) × 1000
Cobb Tester Water Absorption Cobb = Weight After Exposure - Initial Dry Weight
B4 Ford Cup Coating Viscosity Efflux Time in Seconds recorded at 25°C standard.
Moisture Meter Substrate Moisture % • Paper / FBB Target: 6% – 8%
• Corrugated Board Target: 6% – 10%
