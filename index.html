9. Technical Engineering FAQ

Q1: How do you systematically mitigate dot gain during high-speed production?
Ans: By executing meticulous packing under blankets, fine-tuning cylinder impression pressure, and closely auditing the pH and conductivity parameters of the dampening solution.

Q2: What methodology do you deploy to minimize make-ready time?
Ans: Implementing SMED (Single-Minute Exchange of Die) principles, ensuring advanced pre-staging of printing plates, inks, and structural substrates prior to previous job completion.
