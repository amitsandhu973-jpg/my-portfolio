6. Quality Control (QC) Lab Testing & Formulas

Equipment Testing Purpose Parameters & Standard Formulas
Rub / Scuff Tester Ink & Coating Rub Resistance 60 RPM @ 2 lbs weight.
• Aqueous Target: Min 500 rubs
• UV Target: Min 750 rubs
GSM Tester Paper / Board Weight Formula:
GSM = (Weight in Grams / Area in cm²) × 10,000
Taber Stiffness Tester Bending Resistance Formula: Stiffness MD = [(L + R) × 5] / 2
Conversion: mN = 2.04 × Taber Unit
Bursting Strength Tester Substrate Bursting Limit Formula:
Burst Factor = (Bursting Strength / Dry GSM) × 1000
Cobb Tester Water Absorption Capacity Formula:
Cobb Value = Weight (After Absorption) - Initial Weight
B4 Ford Cup Coating Viscosity Measured in Efflux Time (Seconds)
Moisture Meter Substrate Moisture % • Paper/Board Target: 6% – 8%
• Corrugated Board: 6% – 10%
