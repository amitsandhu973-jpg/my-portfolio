3-Cylinder Sheetfed Offset Working Principle

Lithographic Planographic Indirect Image Transfer Physics

Key Technical Highlights:

  • Planographic Principle: Image and non-image areas exist on the same flat plane on the CTP plate.
  • Chemical Immiscibility: Based on mutual repulsion of Water (Hydrophilic) and Oil/Ink (Oleophilic).
  • Indirect Printing: Ink transfers from Plate Cylinder $\rightarrow$ Rubber Blanket Cylinder $\rightarrow$ Paper Substrate.

✅ Advantages (फ़ायदे):

  • Sharp dot reproduction on rough paper due to flexible rubber blanket.
  • High-speed continuous printing ($16,500$ sheets/hr).
  • Extended plate life as plate does not directly touch rough paper.

❌ Disadvantages (सीमाएं):

  • Requires strict chemical control over Dampening Water pH and Conductivity.
  • Risk of Emulsification if Water-Ink balance is disturbed.
  • Higher initial makeready waste compared to digital printing.

Technical Specifications & Working Standards

Plate Cylinder Undercut 0.15 mm (0.006 in) True Rolling Standard
Blanket Cylinder Undercut 2.30 mm (0.091 in) with standard compressibility
Dampening Solution pH Window 4.8 to 5.5 pH at 10°C - 12°C Temperature
Conductivity Target Range 1500 to 2000 µS/cm above raw water baseline
Impression Squeeze Pressure 0.10 mm to 0.12 mm over paper caliper thickness
