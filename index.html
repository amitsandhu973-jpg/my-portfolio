4. Quality Control (QC) Lab Testing & Standard Formulas

EquipmentQuality ParameterCalculation Formula & Operating Standards
Rub / Scuff Tester Ink & Coating Rub Resistance 60 RPM @ 2 lbs weight.
• Aqueous Target: Min 500 rubs
• UV Target: Min 750 rubs
GSM Tester Grammage Weight Formula:
GSM = (Weight in Grams / Area in cm²) × 10,000
Taber Stiffness Tester Bending Resistance Formula:
Stiffness (MD) = [(Left + Right Reading) × 5] / 2
Conversion: mN = 2.04 × Taber Unit
Bursting Strength Tester Board Rupture Limit Formula:
Burst Factor = (Bursting Strength / Dry GSM) × 1000
Cobb Tester Water Absorption Capacity Formula:
Cobb Value = Weight After Absorption - Initial Dry Weight
B4 Ford Cup Coating Viscosity Measured in Efflux Time (Seconds) at 25°C.
Moisture Meter Substrate Moisture % • Paper & FBB Target: 6% – 8%
• Corrugated Fluted Board: 6% – 10%
