Chapter 3: Spectral Science & Color Control

Spectrophotometry: The Science Beyond Delta E

1. D50 Illuminant & The 2-Degree Observer Myth

• The Hidden Logic: Spectrophotometer hamesha "D50" (Daylight 5000K) par set hona chahiye. Lekin asli secret "2° vs 10° Standard Observer" me hai.
• Technical Reality: 2° observer small field of view (up to 4 degrees) ke liye hai, jabki 10° observer larger color patches ke liye. Agar aap 10° observer use kar rahe hain, toh aapki Delta E readings alag aayengi. Pro-tip: Client ki specification check karein, mismatch hone par print kabhi match nahi hoga.

2. M0, M1, M2 & M3: The Measurement Modes

• M1 Mode (The Industry Standard): Aajkal M1 mode zaroori hai kyunki ye "UV content" ko include karta hai.
• Hidden Danger: Agar aapka paper 'Optical Brightening Agents' (OBA) wala hai aur aap M0 mode me measure kar rahe hain, toh aapke prints ki Blue light reading hamesha galat aayegi. M2 mode me UV cut hota hai, jisse OBA ka asar khatam ho jata hai. Hamesha confirm karein ki aapka target M1 me measure hua hai ya M2 me.

3. The Delta E Equation (CIEDE2000 vs 76)

• Physics of Comparison: Delta E 76 (Lab) formula purana hai. Aajkal CIEDE2000 use hota hai.
• The Formula Logic: ΔE00 = √((ΔL'/kLSL)² + (ΔC'/kCSC)² + (ΔH'/kHSH)²)
• Why it matters: CIEDE2000 human eye ki perception ke hisaab se bani hai, jisme Yellow aur Blue color errors ko human eye alag tarah se feel karta hai. Delta E 76 me Yellow error dikhta hi nahi, jabki wo print me bahut kharab lagta hai.

4. Metamerism: When Colors Lie

• The Logic: Metamerism tab hota hai jab do colors ek light source me match karte hain, but dusre me alag.
• Cause: Jab aapka pigment (Colorant) spectral curve dusre ink ke curve ko cross karta hai.
• Technical Secret: Agar machine par shade match nahi ho rahi, toh sirf 'Lab' values mat dekho, 'Spectral Data Curve' dekho. Agar curve cross ho raha hai, toh ink change karo, Lab match karne ki koshish waste hai.
