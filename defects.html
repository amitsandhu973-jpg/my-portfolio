Chapter 4: Print Defects Troubleshooting

15 Common Print Defects: Root Causes & Hidden Mechanics

1. Hickeys: Plate par dust ya dry ink skin ke pieces. Root cause: Ink fountain me skinning ya paper coating dust.
2. Slur: Image double dikhna. Root cause: Blanket aur impression cylinder ke beech 'surface velocity mismatch'. Gear backlash check karein.
3. Doubling: Image ka shadow dikhna. Root cause: Gripper setting kharab hona ya sheet ka cylinder se 'flutter' karna.
4. Ghosting: Light image par dark patches. Root cause: Ink form roller par ink distribution uneven hona (roller timing check karein).
5. Toning/Scumming: Non-image area par ink chadhna. Root cause: Fountain solution ka pH level high hona ya plate ki gumming kharab hona.
6. Picking: Paper ka upar ka layer ukhadna. Root cause: Ink ka tack (stickiness) paper ki strength se zyada hona.
7. Set-off: Sheet ka peeche wali sheet par chhapna. Root cause: Anti-set-off powder kam hona ya stack height bahut zyada hona.
8. Fan-out: Sheet ke corners par register kharab hona. Root cause: Paper ka machine me moisture ki wajah se expand ho jana.
9. Scuffing: Print par scratch aana. Root cause: Delivery me sheets ka aapas me friction (ink abhi tak dry nahi hui hai).
10. Chalking: Ink ka paper par dry na hona (powder ki tarah nikalna). Root cause: Paper ka 'pH' bahut acidic hona jo drier ko deactivate kar deta hai.
11. Drying Problems: Ink ka sukhna nahi. Root cause: Fountain solution emulsification bahut zyada ho jana.
12. Misregistration: Color ka fit na hona. Root cause: Side lay ya front lay ke sensors me ink mist jamna (Encoder check).
13. Mottling: Solid print me 'dhabbe' dikhna. Root cause: Paper ki absorbency uniform na hona ya dampening water bahut zyada hona.
14. Roller Stripping: Ink ka metal roller par na chadhna. Root cause: Dampening water ka ink roll tak pahunch jana (hydrophilic surface).
15. Ink Bleeding: Edge se ink failna. Root cause: Fountain solution me 'Surfactants' ka excess hona.
