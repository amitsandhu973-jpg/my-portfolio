Chapter 4: Critical Print Defects & Floor Troubleshooting Matrix

Sheet-Fed Offset Fault Diagnosis, Mechanical Root Causes, and Instant Corrective Actions

⚙️ 1. Sheet Feeding, Registration & Mechanical Faults (1 to 10)

# Defect Name Root Mechanical Cause Immediate Floor Action
01 Slur / Double Image Blanket loose, improper packing thickness, or gear backlash mismatch. Check blanket torque wrench tension; verify undercut and packing calculation.
02 Side Lay Misregistration Pull lay eye dirty, weak suction, or excessive sheet curl/waviness. Clean fiber optic sensor glass, adjust pull-lay pressure and air blast.
03 Hickies (Ring-like Spots) Paper dust, dried ink skin, or calcium carbonate accumulation on blanket/plate. Wash blanket with approved solvent; check dampening roller condition and paper trimming.
04 Sheet Skew at Front Lay Uneven front-lay tongue pressure or misaligned feeder brush wheels. Re-adjust front-lay fingers equally; balance brush wheel pressure across the sheet.
05 Double Sheet Stoppage Error Double sheet detector sensor out of calibration or greasy ultrasonic eyes. Clean sensor heads with isopropyl alcohol and recalibrate for board thickness.
06 Tail-Edge Flutter / Creasing Improper tail-edge star wheel setting or excessive moisture in paper stock. Adjust deceleration suction wheels and set dynamic sheet tensioners.
07 Gripper Margin Smudge Gripper pads worn out, oil leakage from delivery chain guide, or dirty fingers. Replace damaged gripper pads, clean oil deposits, check torque (18-22 Ncm).
08 Ghosting (Mechanical) Inadequate ink distribution or worn-out form roller nips failing to recharge ink film. Check roller strips (nip width); adjust oscillating travel timing.
09 Web / Sheet Jam in Transfer Drum Incorrect air-guide pressure or timed vacuum release failure. Calibrate Bernoulli air valves and inspect transfer drum timing cams.
10 Static Electricity Sticking Low relative humidity on shop floor (<40% RH) causing sheets to stick together. Activate static eliminator tinsel/bars; increase room moisture via humidifier.

💧 2. Dampening, Toning & Chemical Reaction Faults (11 to 20)

# Defect Name Root Chemical / Press Issue Corrective Countermeasure
11 Scumming / Toning Insufficient fountain solution or high ink feed; pH above 5.5. Increase dampening water feed; adjust pH to 4.8 - 5.2 using additive.
12 Stripping on Steel Rollers Ink not accepting water on steel oscillating rollers due to glazing/gumming. Wash rollers with calcium cleaner or roller rejuvenator gel to restore hydrophilicity.
13 Emulsification Pile-up Excess water mixed into ink (water-in-oil emulsion), causing dull washed-out prints. Reduce dampening feed rate and check ink body/tack strength.
14 Piling on Plate / Blanket Paper fibers or coating pigment accumulating on blankets due to weak surface strength. Use anti-piling blanket wash; check paper pick resistance (Igrotest).
15 Tinting (Non-image area color bleed) Water-soluble fountain solution contamination or aggressive ink pigments. Check chiller tank water conductivity (800-1200 µS/cm); flush damping system.
16 Roller Stripping (Inking side) Hardened ink film glaze on copper/steel rollers. Perform deep cleaning paste application; check roller nip stripe settings.
17 Dampening Roller Slipperiness Alcolor metering roller worn out or chrome roller surface contaminated. Replace metering roller sleeve; clean chrome roller with copper-safe cleaner.
18 Corrosion / Rusting of Plates Fountain solution acidity too high (pH < 4.0) eating away non-image base. Calibrate dosing pump; check gum arabic layer protection on stopped plates.
19 Ink Bleed in Fountain Solution Incompatible dampening additives reacting chemically with organic pigments. Change fountain solution concentrate brand; check IPA substitute ratios.
20 Blistering on Coated Board Excessive IR dryer temperature evaporating moisture too rapidly inside board core. Lower IR lamp intensity; increase hot-air knife airflow volume in delivery.

🔥 3. Set-Off, Drying & Post-Press Hazards (21 to 28)

# Defect Name Core Chemistry / Press Issue Corrective Countermeasure
21 Set-Off (Ink Transfer to Back) High stack pile weight, slow-drying ink, or insufficient anti-setoff powder. Increase spray powder dosage; reduce pile height; add cobalt/manganese dryer paste.
22 Chalking (Rub-Off) Ink vehicle absorbed too fast into porous paper, leaving dry pigment unbound. Change paper grade, use sealer stops, or adjust ink vehicle penetration index.
23 Blocking (Sheets stuck solid) High pile pressure combined with heavy solid coverage and trapped heat. Turn down delivery stack air pressure; split large stacks into smaller pallets.
24 Crystallization / Glassing First color ink dried completely hard and glossy, rejecting subsequent wet ink layers. Add fresh ink compound modifier; print wet-on-wet within 4-hour window or use primer.
25 Picking / Delamination High ink tack exceeding paper surface internal bonding strength. Reduce ink tack using reducer paste; slow down press running speed.
26 Orange Peel Effect (Coatings) Incorrect viscosity or drying speed of aqueous/UV varnish layer. Adjust Anilox roller chamber pressure and check fluid temperature/viscosity.
27 Poor Foil Stamping Adhesion UV varnish cured too hard or surface energy (Dynes) too low before stamping. Optimize UV lamp power; check corona treatment level (>38 dynes/cm).
28 Scuffing during Die-Cutting Incomplete ink drying or inadequate scratch-resistant wax additive in ink formulation. Mix micro-crystalline wax paste into ink; allow complete oxidation cure before conversion.

🎨 4. Color Variation, Dot Gain & Density Faults (29 to 35)

# Defect Name Root Optical / Measurement Cause Corrective Countermeasure
29 Excessive Dot Gain (Dot Spreading) Too much printing pressure, worn blanket compression, or excessive ink feed. Recalibrate packing undercuts; check spectrophotometer target density values.
30 Color Drift Across Sheet Width Ink fountain blade key uneven adjustment or worn ink duct roller eccentric. Calibrate ink key preset profiles; clean ink fountain blade lip and keys.
31 Metamerism (Color Mismatch under D65) Different pigment formulations matching under shop light but failing under lightbox. Reformulate ink batch using standard ISO color index pigments (Process colors).
32 High Delta E (ΔE) Variance Unstable solid ink density (SID) or fluctuating dampening balance during run. Lock spectrophotometer reading limits within ΔE < 2.0 tolerance range.
33 Mottling (Uneven Solid Patch) Paper formation irregularity or poor ink transfer distribution on heavy solids. Use soft blanket packings; adjust form roller strip widths and ink viscosity.
34 Trapping Failure in Multi-Color Units Tack sequence incorrect (earlier ink tack lower than subsequent wet ink). Maintain decreasing tack order from Unit 1 to Unit 4/6 (Black > Cyan > Magenta > Yellow).
35 Plate Wear / Premature Image Fade Excessive abrasive particles in paper/ink or over-scrubbing plate with strong chemicals. Use mild plate cleaner, check water pH balance, and replace worn rubber form rollers.
