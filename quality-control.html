Print Density Targets, Dot Gain Standards, Register Accuracy & Visual Inspection Protocols

Verified By: Amit Sandhu (B.Tech)

📊 1. Solid Ink Density (SID) & ISO 12647-2 Reference Targets

Standard reflection density limits for offset printing on coated art paper (measured via densitometer):

Process Color ISO Status T Density Range Dot Gain Tolerance (50% Dot) Primary Inspection Focus
Cyan (C) 1.30 - 1.40 18% (±4%) Check for slurring and plate wear.
Magenta (M) 1.35 - 1.50 19% (±4%) Monitor emulsification balance.
Yellow (Y) 0.90 - 1.10 19% (±4%) Check transparency and trap value.
Black (K) 1.60 - 1.80 21% (±4%) Critical for fine text sharpness & contrast.

🔍 2. Daily Pressroom Quality Verification Tests

Thumb Nail / Scratch Resistance Test

Checks ink drying and curing adequacy on heavy coverage board. Performed after 15–20 minutes of delivery stacking to ensure no scuffing occurs during post-press turning.

Fountain Solution Conductivity & pH

Must be monitored continuously using a digital meter. Standard dampening water pH: 4.8 – 5.5, and Conductivity: 800 – 1200 µS/cm depending on IPA dosage.

Register & Cross-Perpendicularity Check

Using a 10x illuminated magnifying glass on registration crosshairs at the sheet corners. Ensures multi-color front-to-back and unit-to-unit alignment within 0.02 - 0.04 mm.

Tack-O-Scope & Viscosity Audit

Ensures proper ink tack sequence (Black > Cyan > Magenta > Yellow) to prevent wet-on-wet back-trapping and contamination of ink fountain units.

💡 Amit Sandhu's Quality Control Standard:

Never approve a press run based solely on visual appearance under ambient mobile flashlight or office tube lights. Always evaluate press sheets inside a standardized lighting booth at D50 (5000K daylight) color temperature to accurately detect metamerism, cast shifts, and trap variations before bulk production runs.
