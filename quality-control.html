CHAPTER 6: QUALITY CONTROL & SHOP-FLOOR TESTING

Print Density Targets, Dot Gain Standards, Register Accuracy & Visual Inspection Protocols

Verified By: Amit Sandhu (B.Tech)

📊 1. Solid Ink Density (SID) & ISO Standards (डेंसिटी और टारगेट रेंज)

Coated art paper par offset printing ke dauran standard reflection density limits (जिन्हें densitometer से मापा जाता है):

Process Color ISO Status T Density Range Dot Gain (50% Dot) Primary Inspection Focus
Cyan (C) 1.30 - 1.60 18% (±4%) Slurring aur plate wear check karein.
Magenta (M) 1.30 - 1.60 19% (±4%) Emulsification balance monitor karein.
Yellow (Y) 1.00 - 1.30 19% (±4%) Transparency aur trap value check karein.
Black (K) 1.60 - 1.95 21% (±4%) Fine text sharpness aur contrast ke liye critical.

🔍 2. Daily Pressroom Quality Verification Tests (रोज़ाना होने वाले टेस्ट)

Thumb Nail / Scratch Resistance Test (नखून टेस्ट)

Heavy coverage board par ink ki drying aur curing check karta hai. Delivery stack me sheet lagne ke 15-20 minute baad check karein taaki turning ke waqt scuffing na ho.

Fountain Solution Conductivity & pH

Digital meter se lagataar monitor karein. Standard dampening water pH: 4.8 – 5.5, aur Conductivity: 800 – 1200 µS/cm honi chahiye (IPA dosage ke hisab se).

Register & Cross-Perpendicularity Check

Sheet ke corners par registration crosshairs ko 10x illuminated magnifying glass se check karein. Front-to-back aur unit-to-unit alignment hamesha 0.02 - 0.04 mm ke andar honi chahiye.

Tack-O-Scope & Viscosity Audit

Proper ink tack sequence maintain karein (Black > Cyan > Magenta > Yellow) taaki wet-on-wet back-trapping na ho aur fountain units contaminate na ho.

💡 Amit Sandhu's Quality Control Standard (सुपरवाइज़र टिप):

Kabhi bhi press run ko mobile ki flashlight ya office ki normal tube light me dekh kar approve na karein. Bulk production se pehle metamerism, shade shifts, aur trap variations ko pakadne ke liye hamesha sheets ko D50 (5000K daylight) standard lighting booth ke andar hi evaluate karein.
