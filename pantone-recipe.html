Spot Color Matching, Base Ink Mixing Standards, Spectrophotometer Integration & Delta-E Control

🎨 1. The 14 Standard Pantone Base Inks (Offset Formulation)

Every single Pantone spot color is blended using precise weight percentages from these 14 foundational mixing bases:

1. Yellow Primary bright yellow base
2. Warm Red Red with yellow undertone
3. Rubine Red Deep bluish-red base
4. Rhodamine Red Vibrant magenta-red base
5. Purple Deep violet shade
6. Reflex Blue Intense dark royal blue
7. Process Blue Cyan standard base
8. Green Vivid green mixing base
9. Black High density offset black
10. Transparent White Extender base for tinting
11. Orange Bright orange mixing base
12. Violet Blue-violet base
13. Rhodamine Ext. Specialized modifier base
14. Metallic Bases Silver & Gold pastes

⚖️ 2. Ink Kitchen Formulation & Gravimetric Weighing

When preparing custom Pantone shades on the shop floor:

  • Always use a high-precision digital electronic balance (accuracy up to 0.01 grams).
  • Weigh the major component base first, followed by incremental drops of darker shading bases.
  • Perform a drawdown test on the actual customer substrate (Coated or Uncoated board) using an ink drawdown wire bar applicator.
  • Compare the dried drawdown sheet under a D50 light booth against the official Pantone Formula Guide swatch.
💡 Amit Sandhu's Pantone Matching Tip:

Never evaluate wet ink freshly pulled from an ink knife. Offset inks undergo a slight shift in spectral values and gloss level as they dry and penetrate the paper fibers over 15 to 20 minutes. Always cure or fully dry your drawdown sample before measuring Delta-E ($dE$) with a spectrophotometer.
