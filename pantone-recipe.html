🎨 1. The 14 Standard Pantone Base Inks (Offset Mixing Bases)

Har ek Pantone spot color ko banane ke liye niche diye gaye 14 base inks ko exact weight percentage me mix kiya jata hai:

1. Yellow Primary bright yellow base
2. Warm Red Red with yellow undertone
3. Rubine Red Deep bluish-red base
4. Rhodamine Red Vibrant magenta-red base
5. Purple Deep violet shade
6. Reflex Blue Intense dark royal blue
7. Process Blue Cyan standard base
8. Green Vivid green mixing base
9. Black High density offset black
10. Transparent White Extender base for tinting
11. Orange Bright orange mixing base
12. Violet Blue-violet base
13. Rhodamine Ext. Specialized modifier base
14. Metallic Bases Silver & Gold pastes

⚖️ 2. Ink Kitchen Formulation & Weighing Rules (मिक्सिंग के नियम)

Shop floor par jab bhi koi custom Pantone shade banayein, toh in baton ka dhyan rakhein:

  • Hamesha high-precision digital electronic balance (accuracy up to 0.01 grams) ka use karein taaki formula bilkul accurate bane.
  • Pehle sabse heavy/major component base ko weigh karein, uske baad dark shading bases ki boondein (drops) dhyan se add karein.
  • Customer ke actual substrate (Coated ya Uncoated board) par ink drawdown wire bar applicator se sample zaroor check karein.
  • Dried drawdown sheet ko official Pantone Formula Guide swatch ke sath D50 light booth me match karein.
💡 Amit Sandhu's Expert Tip (मास्टर टिप):

Kabhi bhi geeli (wet) ink ko ink knife se nikal kar turant final approve na karein. Offset inks jab paper ke andar penetrate hoti hain aur sukhti hain, toh unka gloss aur shade thoda change hota hai. Drawdown ko 15-20 minute sukhane ke baad hi Spectrophotometer se Delta-E ($dE$) check karein!
